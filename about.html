Our Story

A shirt is more
than a shirt.

It was worn once. By one player. In one match. That's what makes it unique — not the club, not the competition, not the result. The simple fact that it was there, on a player's back, at a specific moment in time.

These shirts exist. They change hands. But the market is opaque, full of fakes, and without reliable documentation. Most shirts lose their story along the way.

We built curated.football to change that. A specialised marketplace where every shirt is authenticated before it goes live — and where every piece comes with its full story: the player, the match, the moment, the chain of custody.

What We Stand For
01 / Authentication
No shirt without verification
Every shirt is checked before it goes live. MWS/Fabricks auto-verification, recognised COA portals, or direct player provenance. If we can't verify it, it doesn't sell here.
02 / Protection
Collectors deserve a safe market
We arbitrate disputes. We verify certificates. We document every transaction. Whether you're spending €75 or €550, you should know exactly what you're buying.
03 / Access
Every club. Every level.
A shirt from Amiens matters as much as one from Real Madrid. We curate pieces from across European football — from Ligue 2 to the Champions League. If the story is real, it belongs here.
Our Approach

Curation over volume

We don't list everything. Every shirt submitted is reviewed manually. We add context — the match stats, the player's career, the significance of the moment. We assign each piece to an editorial collection.

The goal isn't to be the biggest marketplace. It's to be the most trusted one in European football.
